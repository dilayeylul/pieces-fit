Hello World!

This is me from the 27th of September, 2026. A rainy day in İzmir, my hometown. I am home alone, sitting at my desk and trying to figure out life.

All this time, I waited to feel ready. But then I realized ready will never find me. So, I am going to start anyway.

I have a lot to say. Tons of ideas have been going back and forth in my mind for years now. I have to admit, I still doubt myself. What if things don’t turn out the way I want them to be?

I cannot see the end of the tunnel — in fact, I don't even know if I've actually started to dig my way at all!

Yet all I have is a belief that tells me I can do it.

No, I am going to do it.

No!

I will make it happen no matter what!

And figure out the rest on the road.

Here, in this blog, I plan to pour out whatever storm is happening in my head that day. I will share my struggles and how I chose to sort them out.

I will be real.

As I look back to today from someday in the future, I want to be able to see the path that I created for myself, hopefully feeling proud.

I want to be seen so that I can break out of my shell and set myself free — I will step out even if I feel scared.

And here I am, a beginner on my own road, not afraid of the world judging my faults and mistakes, because I know I will grow!

The Final Frontier

As I stand out here in the wonders of the unknown at Hadley, I sort of realize there’s a fundamental truth to our nature, Man must explore, and this is exploration at its greatest.
